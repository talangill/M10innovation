👁

Empathize

Understand needs and perspectives of clients and stakeholders

5 Written Test Q's
Before you can solve anything, you need to deeply understand the people you're designing for. This phase is about curiosity — go talk to real people, observe, listen without judging.
📍

Define

Create a clear, impactful problem statement translated into a meaningful challenge

5 Written Test Q's
Turn your research into a sharp, focused challenge. A great problem statement is the difference between a concept that hits and one that misses. Take your time here.
💡

Ideate

Generate diverse and innovative ideas using design-thinking techniques

5 Written Test Q's
No bad ideas — yet. This is the most creative phase. Generate widely before you narrow. Quantity leads to quality. The best ideas usually come after the obvious ones.
🔧

Prototype

Develop tangible representations of concepts for testing

5 Written Test Q's
Make your idea real enough to test. A prototype doesn't need to be perfect — it needs to be testable. Cheap, fast, and scrappy beats polished but untested.
✅

Test

Collect feedback systematically and analyze results

5 Written Test Q's
Get your assumptions in front of real people and let reality reshape your concept. The goal isn't validation — it's learning. Surprises here are gold.

5-minute pitch structure

  1. Opening hook (~30 sec) — compelling question, quote, or statistic
  2. Context & opportunity (~1 min) — strategic problem, key stakeholders
  3. The concept (~2 min) — what it is, why best, research support, key visuals
  4. Implementation plan (~1 min) — changes, who's involved, timeline / Service Blueprint
  5. Costs & benefits (~1 min) — environmental, social, financial impacts + 3-year forecast
  6. Risks & success measurement (~1 min) — KPIs, mitigation strategies

How the Calculation Works

3-step process — each step is input for the next

Step 1 — Word Count: The Business Case max is 4,000 words. Every 3% excess deducts 0.1 from your oral score first.

Step 2 — Business Case multiplier: Score 2 → final grade 1.0, oral denied. Score 4 → ×0.8. Score 6 → no change. Score 8 → ×1.1 (max 10). Score 10 → ×1.2 (max 10).

Step 3 — Individual Contribution: Score 4 → −1.0. Score 6 → no change. Score 8 → +0.5 (max 10). Score 10 → +1.0 (max 10).
